Changes – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Changes is an I Ching reading app. This policy explains what stays on your device and what is sent to our servers. Contact: open an issue on our support page (please do not post private information).

What stays on your device

Your questions, coin tosses, readings and journal are stored only on your device. We have no access to them. There are no sign-ups, advertising, analytics or trackers in the app.

What is sent when you tap “Explain with AI”

Only when you tap the button and confirm, the app sends your question, any context you wrote, and the cast (the six line values and the passages being read) to our server. Our server forwards it to an AI service (OpenRouter and the model provider it selects, requested with data collection disabled) to write the explanation, and returns the reply to your device. We do not store your question, context or the AI reply on our server. Do not include sensitive personal information in your question.

What our server keeps

  • A random device identifier and a secret token created when the app first needs credits. The token is also saved in your device Keychain, so credits survive reinstalling the app. Only a hash of the token is kept on the server.
  • Your credit balance, subscription expiry, and a log of credit changes (purchases, uses, referral rewards) so we can keep the balance correct and prevent abuse.
  • A hashed IP address, kept per day, to limit abuse when new installs are created.
  • If you enter or share a referral code: which code was used. Codes are random and do not identify you.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple. We receive a signed transaction from Apple (product, transaction identifier and dates) to add credits or activate your plan. We never see your payment details or Apple ID.

Sharing with others

We do not sell your data. Service providers that process data for us: Supabase (hosting and database), OpenRouter and the AI model provider it routes to (to write explanations), and Apple (payments).

Your choices

Using the app without tapping “Explain with AI” sends nothing about your readings. To ask us to delete the data tied to your device identifier, open an issue titled “Delete my data” and include your referral code (shown in Credits). Your referral code is not secret and does not identify you. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We will update the date above when this policy changes.
